## What the two modes establish

**The forward pass is correct.** With the golden's routing the engine reproduces the reference
through all 40 layers at the fp16 floor — mHC at 1e-7, attention and experts at 2-5e-4 through
the ratio-2 layers, 1e-3 through the ratio-1 layers — and the logits at 1.07e-3. Every
mechanism a text prefill uses now runs on device and matches: both compressor ratios, index
sources with and without their own keys, consumers of each cache, engram at both layers, the
collapse with the last layer's `pre`, the head.

**Routing flips are the only divergence, and they are near-ties.** The `rdiff` and `flip gap`
columns locate it exactly: layer 7, one token, golden margin 6.87e-5 against ~5e-4 of
accumulated stream error. This is the MoE router's version of what the layer-2 indexer showed
in 6b, and it was proved the same way — by forcing the selection and watching the error
collapse to the floor. The reference's own quantised kernels would flip the same ties.

## The residency planner's starting line

Per-layer streaming moved **29.2 GiB** for a 12-token prefill (27.8 before the gate found the head, final norm and the true device size of fp32 uploads missing from the count): ~330 MB of dense weights plus
20-44 routed experts (5.3-9.7 MiB each with scales... 17.93 MiB per expert across three banks)
per layer. Every byte of that is re-uploaded on every forward. Phase 8 makes the dense weights
and as many experts as fit resident, which is what turns 10 s into the projected 6-9 tok/s.

## The decode golden exists

`golden_decode.py` keeps all 40 reference blocks alive (memmap-backed experts, so 40 live
blocks cost nothing for the 269 GB of experts) and runs the reference's own generate loop —
a prefill, then single tokens at `start_pos = n` with every cache carried: window rings,
compressed KV at the compressor's cadence, index keys, the engram hash cache.

```
generated: ' Berlin. The capital of'
full: 'The capital of France is Paris. The capital of Germany is Berlin. The capital of'
```

Per-step logits and the first decode step's per-layer streams are dumped: Phase 9's target.

## Named gaps carried forward

- two-level candidate top-k above 16,384 compressed positions (the runtime refuses, loudly);
- the `-1e30` attention floor's fully-invalid-row case (cannot occur in decode either — the
  current token is always present — so it stays a non-case, stated rather than claimed);
- tokenisation inside the engine: the V4-Flash GGUF vocabulary is the same BPE (129,280 ids,
  merges identical in order, bos/eos 0/1) with **nine special-token strings** differing at ids
  >= 128,799 (`<|System|>`, `<|deepseek_image|>`, placeholder renames), so `tokenizer_ref_gguf`
  serves V4.1 with a nine-entry override.

## Phase 7 gate: PASS, eight non-blocking findings, all acted on (2026-09-12)

The gate judged the amendment principled on three independently verified grounds: forced mode
is own-router mode minus exactly two lines (`force_routing` appears at 38 and 319; the router
runs unconditionally); forced mode bites — its stale-cache mutant was caught at the injected
layer, 7.1e-2 against 5e-3, with layers 0-8 bit-identical; and the engine's own router agrees
with the reference at 39 of 40 layers on a golden-tracking stream, including layers 14, 15 and
21 whose tightest margins are *smaller* than layer 7's and did not flip. The flipped token was
the tightest tie in its layer, and the gate measured the conversion my doc had fudged: 5e-4 of
stream error moves a selection margin by median 5.5e-5, p99 2.35e-4 — the layer-7 flip sits
below the median, and the 3e-3 band is ~9x the p99.
