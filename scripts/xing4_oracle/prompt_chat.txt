I run a small bakery and I'm trying to decide whether to buy a second commercial oven or hire an extra baker for the early shift. Here is my situation in detail.

We open at 6:30 in the morning, six days a week. Right now one baker comes in at 3:00 and handles everything: the sourdough loaves, the baguettes, croissants, muffins and two kinds of scones. Our single deck oven holds about 24 loaves at a time, and a sourdough bake takes roughly 45 minutes including the steam phase. On weekdays we sell around 90 loaves, 60 croissants and maybe 80 pastries of other kinds. On Saturdays that nearly doubles, and we regularly sell out of sourdough by 10:00, which customers complain about.

The oven I'm looking at costs about $18,000 installed, would need an electrical upgrade of around $3,500, and the dealer says it would last fifteen years with normal maintenance. An extra baker would cost me roughly $22 an hour for a five-hour shift, so about $570 a week including taxes and benefits. My current baker says the real bottleneck is the oven, not her hands, but she is also exhausted and has hinted that she might leave if Saturdays stay this busy.

My margins on bread are about 65 percent and on pastries closer to 70 percent. I have about $25,000 in savings set aside for the business, and I'd rather not take a loan. I also have a small walk-in cooler that is already almost full, so if we bake more we'd need to think about where the dough retards overnight.

Can you walk me through how to think about this decision? I'd like a rough payback calculation for each option, the risks I might be missing, and whether there is a third option I haven't considered. Please be concrete with the numbers where you can, and tell me which option you would lean toward and why.
